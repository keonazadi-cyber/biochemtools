What other people asked for, and what happened

Every name here is someone who sent a note and changed the site. Several of them are credited in the footers of the charts they fixed.

The buffer chart was rebuilt four times, by four different people

u/erikna10 on r/Biochemistry asked for piperazine and the charge of each buffering ion, so the table could be used to plan ion exchange runs. The buffer ion must not stick to the resin.

u/MorphingSp on r/Biochemistry asked for polyprotic buffers on a single row with each pKa marked. Citrate's ranges overlap into one bar, phosphate's stay separate islands, because drawing phosphate as unbroken from pH 1 to 13 would be a lie.

u/RendertheFatCap on r/Biochemistry pointed out that the crowd of buffers near pH 7 is a property of the list rather than of chemistry. The footer now says so outright.

u/orchidguy on r/Biochemistry said the pKa was stranded on the far side of the chart, so your eye had to cross the whole width to connect a buffer to its number. It sits next to the name now.

Mixed inhibition was wrong, and a reader said so

u/xtalgeek on r/Biochemistry pointed out the chart showed one case of mixed inhibition as though it were the rule. Mixed inhibition is a continuum. It is drawn as two rows now, and the correction is credited in the chart's own footer.

A whole chart exists because two comments said the old one was wrong for studying

r/Mcat on on the original biochem chart "all this info and no structures" and "way too much detail". So molecular weight and hydropathy came out, every amino acid got drawn, and the DEHCYKR mnemonic came from a commenter there too.
